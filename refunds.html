Refund Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Digital goods

Because templates are digital goods that can be accessed or downloaded immediately, refunds are generally not available after delivery, except where required by applicable law.

EU consumer rights

EU consumers normally have a 14-day right of withdrawal for online purchases. For digital content, this right may end when delivery begins if you expressly consented to immediate delivery and acknowledged that you would lose the right of withdrawal. Where the law does not permit that exception, your statutory rights remain unaffected.

Faulty or misdescribed products

If a product is materially defective or does not match its description, contact support@example.com within 14 days with your order details. We will investigate and, where appropriate, repair, replace, or refund the product.

Requesting help

Please describe the issue and include your purchase email. Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer protections.
